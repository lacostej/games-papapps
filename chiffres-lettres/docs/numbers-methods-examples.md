# Numbers round: method examples

Under the rule in [numbers-methods.md](numbers-methods.md), one last step often splits into several methods: in 50.8% of kids' draws and 71.0% of normal draws, at least one last step (such as 180 + 7) is reached by two or more methods. An exactly solvable kids' draw has 6.8 methods over 5.8 last steps on average; a normal draw has 13.1 methods over 8.4 last steps (482 and 186 exact draws).

In each table below, a **bold** last step is one that splits: the rows under it are the methods the rule tells apart. Each draw lists every exact solution. The examples are limited to draws with 3 to 12 methods, so the normal ones are smaller than average. `node tools/numbers-methods.mjs kids 7 6` and `node tools/numbers-methods.mjs normal 11 6` regenerate them.
